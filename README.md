## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression enhancements for tactical missions, squad management, and the strategic layer aboard The Den. It is designed for the single-player campaign and supports the game's action points, Advantage points, health, equipment, credits, Intel, and permadeath systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed campaign, faster squad progression, easier experimentation with operator loadouts, or additional flexibility while replaying tactical missions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

In tactical missions, each operator normally has three action points that can be spent on movement and other actions. Enable Infinite Action Points when you want to reposition freely, reach cover, test routes, or combine movement with attacks. Story missions also include short third-person free-roam sections; trainer movement options do not replace the game's normal traversal controls.

### Tools & Inventory

Equipment, loadouts, credits, and operator customization are managed primarily through The Den and the campaign progression systems. Use Unlimited Credits to purchase gear and experiment with different specializations. Apply progression features before changing equipment, then allow the game to save normally before closing the trainer.

### World & Environment

The Den is Zero Company's base of operations, where you recruit and manage operators, upgrade facilities, purchase equipment, and select missions from the Holotable. Operations on the Galaxy Map consume Intel and can provide rewards, narrative developments, and effects on character bonds. Unlimited Intel is useful for exploring optional Operations without restricting normal campaign choices.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and built around action points, cover, ranged and melee attacks, Overwatch, Call for Backup, Rally, and Advantage-powered special actions. Infinite Health protects operators, Unlimited Advantage Points supports frequent special abilities, and Instant Rally helps recover downed squad members. If No Permadeath is enabled, operators remain available even when the campaign would normally remove them permanently.
